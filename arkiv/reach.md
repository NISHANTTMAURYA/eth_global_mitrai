## 1. Target Audience

1. **Web3 Creators & Collectors:**
   - Individuals seeking one-of-a-kind digital beings whose personalities, speech patterns, and 3D forms evolve uniquely based on shared experiences.
   - Collectors who value verifiable on-chain provenance and secondary adoption markets.

2. **Decentralized Communities & DAOs:**
   - DAOs and developer networks looking for autonomous digital representatives and community mascots that accumulate credentials and event memories over time.

3. **Gamers & Virtual World Explorers:**
   - Players looking for companions with persistent identities that can travel between decentralized worlds and platforms via ENS Agent Passports and open Arkiv resolution.

## 2. Economic & Adoption Thesis

- **The Value of Time Capital:**  
  Because MitrAI companions have a mortal lifespan (30, 90, or 180 days), attention is precious. A companion cultivated over 50 meaningful sessions with rich public memory crystals commands genuine secondary adoption demand.
- **Real-World Utility:**  
  Verified community memberships and hackathon event histories unlock exclusive perks:
  - VIP concert and festival access for music community members.
  - Closed-door founder meetups for startup network companions.
  - Ecosystem airdrops and loyalty discounts for verified conference attendees.
